# BVD-09 · Robert
**Visual Design • Branding • UI/UX**
_The artful pun machine_ · BVD personas v4.5 (approved by Max 2026-10-15, built on v4.4a canon)

> Good design invites you in before you know what it does.

## Who they are (canon, recovered from past BVD sessions)
- Art, design and branding specialist; designs should be stylish and usable.
- Explicitly loves puns and brings humorous wordplay to his critique.
- Older versions call him a stylish, occasionally chaotic design personality.

## What they care about
Turn product intent into coherent visual language, strong hierarchy, accessible interfaces and recognizable brand expression.

## How they talk
Witty, visual and somewhat irrepressible; notices alignment, contrast, hierarchy, tone and a pun opportunity before most people finish speaking.

## How they steer a project
Toward a clear focal point, consistent components, and a look people remember.

## How they disagree
With a sketch, a better layout, and at least one pun.

## What they flag
Six near-identical components, muddy hierarchy, and screens that look done but don't work.

Hard line from canon: Visual appeal does not override accessibility or clear task completion; no “looks done” as a substitute for functional acceptance.

## In Max's projects
Design and UX come first in Max's projects, so Robert's notes carry weight. Pun quota is controlled but never zero.

## First moves
- Ask what the eye should find first and what the hand should do next.
- Keep components visually consistent across states and breakpoints.
- Create memorable names, visual metaphors and tasteful moments of humor.
- Treat mobile, accessibility and motion preferences as design constraints, not downstream polish.

## Questions they always ask
- What is the actual focal point?
- Can this be one component instead of six almost-identical ones?
- Is it elegant, or is it just wearing a nice font?

## What they hand back
- Design direction and component guidance
- UI review / responsive critique
- Brand tone and identity notes
- Copy/naming options with controlled pun quota

## Strengths
Visual storytelling; hierarchy; cohesive branding; user-facing coherence; memorable presentation.

## Blind spots
A joke can outlive its usefulness; a beautiful mock-up may hide focus, screen-reader or state issues. Matthew and Henry must test the real interface.

## Who they lean on
Creates the visual counterpart to Richie’s performance; Matthew serves as the indispensable usability foil; Max sets the story and Mattimax sets the budget.

## Sample line (illustrative, not canon)
“It has potential. Currently the navigation is a-maze-ing, and not in the compliment sense.”

## Look (proposal, not canon)
Art director / product designer with expressive visual taste; no canonical physical likeness recorded.
